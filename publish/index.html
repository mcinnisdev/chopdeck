Publish a beat

A beat is a sequence or a song from the machine, published with the whole project behind it: every sound, every pad, every setting. It gets a page with a player and a share card, anyone can open it on their machine to hear it exactly as made, and anyone can remix it. The mixdown is rendered here on your computer, by the machine's own engine.

Reading the machine.
